### 5. Non-Adjacent Frame Continuity References

**Current behavior (understood):** The camera angle change reference feature uses an adjacent previous/next frame as a guiding reference image for generation.

**The problem:** Many shot sequences aren't linear in terms of visual continuity. A shot-reverse-shot pattern, for example, alternates between two camera positions — meaning shot 1, 3, and 5 share a reference, and shots 2, 4, and 6 share a different one. Adjacent frame references won't capture this.

#### Proposed solution:
- Allow users to **manually link non-adjacent frames** as continuity references for each other
- Example: Shot 1B and Shot 1E are linked — they reference each other for generation consistency even though they're not next to each other in the shot list
- This enables proper **shot-reverse-shot**, recurring camera positions, and any other non-linear continuity needs
- Extend and validate the existing camera angle reference mechanism to support this many-to-many linking model
